# Las Vegas Scenic Marathon — draft plan

- **Date:** 2027-02-21 (race day), 5:30 PM night start
- **Participants:** 30,000 runners in 4 waves
- **Distance:** 26.2 miles (42.195 km), certified loop

## Route
Start line and finish line at the Las Vegas Convention Center. The route runs south on the Strip past the Bellagio fountains, loops around the Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign, returns north past the Sphere and finishes after a Fremont Street segment.

## Logistics
- Water station every 2 miles (13 total), plus electrolytes at miles 10, 16 and 22
- Medical tent at miles 6, 13, 20 and a large first aid area at the finish
- Chip timing with mats at start, finish and every 5 km
- 600 course marshals; portable toilets at every water station

## Safety
- Emergency vehicle crossings every 2 miles; ambulance staging at miles 8, 18, 24
- Hospital detour routes for Sunrise and UMC published in advance
- Evacuation plan coordinated with Metro Police

## Community
- Cheer zones in Downtown and the Arts District; resident notice 4 weeks ahead

## Budget
- Cost: USD 5.4M (road closures, police, medical, timing, medals)
- Revenue: USD 6.1M (registration USD 3.9M, sponsors USD 1.8M, merchandise USD 0.4M)

## Timeline
- Setup from 6 AM race day; teardown complete by 3 AM next day
