# San Pedro Sula Scenic Marathon — draft plan

- **Date:** 2027-03-14 (race day), 5:00 AM start to beat the heat
- **Participants:** 10,000 runners in 3 waves
- **Distance:** 26.2 miles (42.195 km), certified loop

## Route
Start line and finish line at Estadio Olímpico Metropolitano. The route runs into downtown past Parque Central and the Cathedral, loops through the Guamilito market area and returns along the main boulevards to the stadium.

## Logistics
- Water station every 2.5 km (16 total), with electrolytes and sponges from km 20
- Medical tent at km 10, 21 and 32, plus a large first aid area at the finish
- Chip timing with mats at start, finish and every 5 km
- 350 course marshals; portable toilets at every water station

## Safety
- Emergency vehicle crossings every 3 km; ambulance staging at km 15, 30 and 38
- Hospital detour routes for Hospital Mario Catarino Rivas and Hospital Leonardo Martínez published in advance
- Evacuation plan coordinated with the municipal police and fire department
- Heat protocol: race stops if the heat index goes above the agreed limit

## Community
- Cheer zones in Barrio Guamilito and around Parque Central; resident notice 4 weeks ahead

## Budget
- Cost: USD 1.3M (road closures, police, medical, timing, medals)
- Revenue: USD 1.6M (registration USD 0.8M, sponsors USD 0.65M, merchandise USD 0.15M)

## Timeline
- Setup from 2 AM race day; roads reopen by 12 PM
